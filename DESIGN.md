# AthleteLoad frontend direction

Owner-selected direction: sports performance editorial, ivory, charcoal, burnt orange, strong typography.

Reading this as a football performance research workspace for coaches, analysts and curious readers, with the visual language of a sports journal. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Decisions and their purpose

- Ivory paper (`#f4f1ea`) and charcoal ink (`#242823`) make dense controls and evidence readable in a desk or training-ground setting. The owner selected a fixed light editorial treatment.
- Burnt orange (`#ab3f24`) anchors the title, section numbering and upward contributions; muted green is reserved for lower contributions and recovery states. Status always includes text.
- Barlow Condensed gives the masthead, headlines and large numbers the compact rhythm of sports editorial typography. DM Sans provides more open letterforms for small controls and research tables.
- A two-column lab keeps inputs next to their consequences. Mobile stacks inputs before results; medium screens reduce spacing before changing the composition.
- Large type gives the introduction a focal point. Rules and numbered sections organize the rest of the page without a repeated card grid.
- Spacing follows the work: compact within a control group, wider between experiments, history and evidence.
- Flat surfaces separate the ratio, advisory and chart. No glow, glass, ornamental grid or floating card shadows are used.
- Existing scenario controls, athlete histories, research tables and optional 3D view define the navigation and content. No invented people, data, testimonials or product claims.
- The product name is text, not an invented logo. Directional arrows identify external links and file downloads only.
- The existing 3D body is an optional conceptual illustration, not a localization of injury risk. It loads when opened and does not auto-rotate.
- Motion is limited to hover feedback and native scroll behavior. Reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling; charts draw without animation.
- The browser simulator retains the existing illustrative formulas and explicitly labels them. Exported model benchmarks are distinct from simulator outputs.

## Responsive behavior

- Wide: split lab, side-by-side research context and evidence, inline navigation.
- Medium: tighter columns, smaller labels, wrapped scenario toolbar.
- Narrow: full-width navigation row, two-by-two scenario controls, stacked lab and research, contained horizontal table scrolling.
- No fixed header or footer hides content. Visible keyboard focus and a skip link are provided.
